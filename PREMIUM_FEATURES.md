# Kitte (切手) - Premium Export Features & Monetization Pipeline

Tying these export capabilities to a monetization strategy is a smart way to monetize **Kitte (切手)** without destroying the fun, tactile sandbox experience that attracts users in the first place.

If we lock basic single exports behind a paywall, casual creators will bounce immediately. Instead, we keep single creation completely friction-free (with 300 DPI exports free for everyone), and monetize **high-utility, bulk production, and professional finish tiers**—a classic "Crafter vs. Studio" model.

---

### The Pricing Model (Prices TBD)
*   **[FREE // CRAFTER DESK]**: Unlimited studio access. Single item export (Standard Fold & Glue / Pro Duplex PDF + Web PNG). **Full 300 DPI production resolution included for free.**
*   **[PRO // CURATOR PASS]** (Monthly Subscription or "Day Pass" Micro-Checkout): Unlocks all 4 advanced premium export formats.
*   **[LIFETIME / MASTER PRESS]** (One-Time Founding Member): Everything in Pro forever, plus unlimited cloud vault storage for saved templates and memory drafts.

---

### The 4 Premium Export Features

#### 1. The "Batch Gang Sheet" (Multi-Item Grid Export)
Instead of printing just one ticket or small card onto an entire A4/Letter sheet (which wastes 80% of the paper), this offers an **Auto-Imposition / Gang Run Sheet**.
*   **How It Works:** The export engine optimally packs 4 to 6 artifacts (ticket stubs, photo strips, travel IDs) onto a single sheet of cardstock. Users will be able to create multiple products in one place across different studios and print them all on a single sheet.
*   **Premium Value:** Users can print their entire memory archive, a weekend trip set, or copies for friends in one print run. It will include professional dashed scissor cut-guides and corner registration lines between the slots, creating the authentic look of an uncut sticker or stamp sheet.

#### 2. Apple Wallet / Google Wallet Pass Export (`.pkpass`)
For digital-first users who appreciate the collectible artifact aesthetic but want modern utility.
*   **How It Works:** Alongside the physical paper PDF, the export pipeline generates a native **"Save to Wallet"** `.pkpass` file for items like the *Memory Ticket Stub* or the *Tyler Travel ID*.
*   **Premium Value:** Translates the physical ticket into a native, scannable pass on their smartphone. It can display live barcode/QR code data, custom seat/event details, and retro branding directly inside the native wallet app. Users will be able to link, download, and add the product directly into their own websites or apps.

#### 3. Split "Perforated Sleeve" / Envelope Template Export
Rather than exporting just the raw ticket, this feature provides the complete artifact packaging experience.
*   **How It Works:** The export generates an additional 1-page PDF containing a matching printable, foldable paper sleeve or mini airmail envelope template.
*   **Premium Value:** Turns a simple flat printout into an assembled, three-dimensional physical keepsake. The envelope will be generated in the same color palette as the artifact, featuring the Kitte logo, dedicated areas for signatures or notes, and precisely dashed cutout lines so users can easily print, cut, fold, and assemble it by themselves.

#### 4. Interactive 3D Web Embed & Animated Glitch/Lenticular GIF
For users who want to showcase their creations on social media, Instagram Stories, or personal portfolios without printing.
*   **How It Works:** 
    *   **"Card Flip / Tilt" GIF/Video Export:** Renders an animated MP4/GIF where the card hovers in 3D space, tilts dynamically to simulate light catching the paper texture, flips to reveal the back side, and loops perfectly.
    *   **Direct Web Archive Link (`kitte.art/v/{id}`):** Generates a lightweight, shareable link with an interactive Three.js/CSS 3D model that recipients can drag, rotate, and flip on their phone screens.
*   **Premium Value:** Maximizes virality and social sharing potential for the platform.

### How to Introduce Pricing Naturally in the Tactile Desk UI
Do not use a generic modern SaaS pricing table. Keep it completely in-universe:
*   **The "Membership Card / Studio Pass":** Present the pricing tier as a weathered library card, gold-foil inspection tag, or stamped metal card resting on the desk corner.
*   **Tactile Upgrade Hook:** When a free user clicks "Batch Gang Sheet" or "Export 3D Flip GIF", open a drawer with an ink-stamped paper ledger showing what the Pass unlocks, complete with a wax seal CTA button.
