# 🎟️ Studio 1: Memory Ticket Stub - Implementation Plan

## 1. Architectural Layout

### The Studio Shell
- **Universal Header:** A reusable top bar. Left side features the massive bold title ("MEMORY TICKET STUB") and a "← Back to Desk" button. Right side features a typewriter-font description.
- **Dual-Panel System:**
  - **Right Panel (The Canvas):** A 3D-perspective drafting table holding the interactive ticket. It has dynamic measurement lines wrapping the outside bounds.
  - **Left Panel (The Control Board):** A fixed-width neo-brutalist control panel. Its contents will **dynamically swap** based on which side of the ticket you are looking at.

### The Interaction Engine (Auto-Flip)
- **Double-Tap to Flip:** Users can simply double-click the ticket on the canvas. 
- The ticket will physically rotate 180° in 3D space.
- As it rotates, the Left Panel will smoothly crossfade its controls from "Front Side Settings" to "Back Side Settings".

---

## 2. The Procedural Ticket Engine

Based on your reference images, we will build a powerful **SVG Masking Engine**. This means no downloaded PNGs. The ticket shapes are generated by code, ensuring they remain perfectly crisp at 300 DPI.

### Structural Varieties (The Cutouts)
We will build a "Shape" selector with the following procedural masks:
1. **Classic Notch:** Standard rectangle with half-circle cutouts exactly at the perforation line.
2. **Vintage Zig-Zag:** The left and right outer edges feature a sharp, jagged sawtooth cut.
3. **Cinema Stub:** Rounded corners with a line of tiny, punched-out perforated holes separating the stub.

### Color & Border Varieties
- **Paper Colors:** A curated palette inspired by your references (Warm White, Vintage Yellow, Kraft Brown, Charcoal Black).
- **Border Styles:** Options for Thick Marker (neo-brutalist), Thin Elegant, or No Border.

### Format Variations
- **Horizontal vs Vertical:** A toggle that instantly reshapes the ticket layout and moves the tear-away stub from the side to the bottom.

---

## 3. The Content & Data (Front vs Back)

The ticket is split into two physical zones: The **Main Body** and the **Tear-away Stub**.

### Front Side (The Visual)
*Controls available in the left panel when viewing the front:*
- **Main Image Upload:** A dedicated zone on the Main Body to upload the core memory photograph. We will apply CSS filters (like grayscale or high-contrast) to match the sketchy vibe.
- **Location & Date:** Hand-written fonts overlaying the image or beside it.
- **Layout Controls:** Shape, Color, Border, and Format selection.

### Back Side (The Details & Validation)
*Controls available in the left panel when viewing the back:*
- **The Barcode:** Fixed specifically on the **Tear-away Stub** section. You can type a string and it will render a real, scannable barcode.
- **Memory Note:** A longer text area for journaling, placed on the Main Body.
- **Signature / Name:** A cursive sign-off.
- **Kitte Stamp:** A toggle to add the official Kitte logo watermark to the background.
